Privacy Policy

Philips Autos, 211 N. Galloway Ave., Mesquite, TX 75149. Effective October 10, 2026.

What we collect

When you book a visit or bring a vehicle to us, we collect your name, mobile phone number, the year, make, model and VIN of your vehicle, and any notes you give us about the problem. During service we may take photos and video of your vehicle and record the repairs we recommend and the work you approve.

How we use it

We use this information to schedule and complete your service, to send you estimates, photos and updates, to send invoices and receipts, and to answer your questions. We do not sell your information.

Text messages

If you give us permission, we send text messages about your visit: appointment confirmations and reminders, repair estimates with photos and video, status updates, and invoices. We do not send marketing texts, and we do not share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. See our Text Messaging Terms.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run our business, such as a text-messaging provider, a hosting and storage provider, and a payment processor. They may only use your information to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep service records and related photos for as long as we need them to support your vehicle's service history, to meet legal and tax requirements, and to resolve disputes.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or stop texts, by calling (972) 285-7760 or emailing philipsautos@hotmail.com. Some records must be kept by law.
